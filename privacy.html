Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how Chulha Technologies handles information on chulha.store, including our internal team tools such as the task tracker at chulha.store/tasks.

Who this applies to

The public website can be visited by anyone. Our internal tools can only be used by people we have personally invited. Everyone else is turned away at sign-in.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use these only to confirm you are on our team and to show your name inside the tool.
  • Work you add to our tools, such as tasks, comments and files.
  • If you email us, your email address and the contents of your message.

We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

Only to run our tools and to reply to you. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build marketing profiles.

Where it is stored

Information is stored securely with our service providers: Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (sign-in). They process it only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are part of the team or as long as we need it for our work. Deleted tasks are removed for good after 7 days. When someone leaves, we remove their access and can delete their account details on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to us.
